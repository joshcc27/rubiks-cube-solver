    About the solver

    Solve runs a small neural network in your browser. It learned by scrambling cubes at random and predicting which move undoes the last turn: 38.7 million examples, one hour of training on a laptop CPU, 2.3 million parameters.

    On its own the network only solves short scrambles. A beam search keeps the 512 most promising move sequences, extends each by one move at a time, and stops when one solves the cube.

    In two test runs of 50 random cubes each, it solved all 100, averaging 31 and 33 moves. The shortest solution for a random cube is usually about 18 moves, so the network's solutions are valid but long.
